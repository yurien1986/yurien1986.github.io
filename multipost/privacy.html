MultiPost Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Data we access

  • TikTok (scopes user.info.basic, video.list): your TikTok open ID and display name, and metadata of your own public videos (video ID, title/caption, duration, creation time, share link, dimensions).
  • Instagram (instagram_business_basic, instagram_business_content_publish): your Instagram professional account ID and username, and the IDs, status and links of Reels that MultiPost publishes for you.
  • Your uploads: the original video files and captions you upload to MultiPost.
  • OAuth tokens issued by TikTok and Meta so MultiPost can act on your behalf.

How we use it

Only to recognise your newly published TikTok videos, match them with the originals you uploaded, and publish those originals to your own Instagram account. No advertising, profiling, analytics or sale of data.

Storage and security

Data is stored in a private database and private cloud storage (Google Cloud). OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM. Access is restricted to the developer.

Sharing

Data is not shared with anyone. Videos and captions are sent only to Instagram, at your request.

Retention and deletion

Uploaded videos are kept until you delete them or they have been published and are no longer needed. Revoking MultiPost's access in TikTok or Instagram settings stops all access. To have all stored data deleted, contact the developer below and it will be removed within 30 days.
