MultiPost

MultiPost is a private, single-user tool that helps one creator cross-post her own short videos. After she publishes a video on TikTok, MultiPost reads the public metadata of her own videos (ID, caption, duration, publish time) to recognise the new post, then publishes the original video file she uploaded to her own Instagram account as a Reel.

It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's accounts.
